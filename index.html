How we are different

Helping UK growers find skilled hands, and skilled hands find honest work.

EcoEarth Ltd connects experienced agricultural workers from around the world with UK growers, packhouses and food producers that hold a valid sponsor licence. We handle the sourcing, the screening and the paperwork, so both sides start on solid ground.

Most of the people we place have spent years in the fields and are completely new to the UK system. Most of the growers we work with know their crop inside out and have never hired from overseas before. The gap between those two positions is where we do our work.

More about us

How we operate. EcoEarth Ltd is a recruitment agency. We do not sponsor visas ourselves — we connect skilled workers with UK employers who hold a valid Home Office sponsor licence, and that employer issues the Certificate of Sponsorship. We will always tell you clearly who your employer would be before you commit to anything.

The process

A clear process, from first call to first day.

Recruiting across borders has a lot of moving parts. We keep it to three stages so everyone knows where they stand.

STAGE 01

Understand the role

We map the job, the skills required, the shift pattern and the salary band, and confirm the role fits an eligible occupation code.

STAGE 02

Source and screen

We search our worldwide network, verify qualifications and references, assess English, and shortlist only those who genuinely match.

STAGE 03

Onboard and settle

We support both sides through documentation, travel and arrival, then stay in touch through the first months.
