About us

Built around one simple idea.

International hiring is still far harder than it needs to be, for the employer and for the worker. EcoEarth Ltd exists to close that gap.

Who we are

One industry, done properly.

EcoEarth Ltd is an international recruitment agency working with growers, packhouses and food producers across the United Kingdom. We source experienced agricultural workers worldwide and introduce them to UK businesses that hold a valid Home Office sponsor licence.

One industry, one country, and a process we can explain in plain language to anyone who asks. We would rather make fewer placements that last than many that fall apart in the first six months.

Everything we do runs on one principle: nobody should sign anything they do not fully understand.

What we stand for

Four things we do not compromise on.

01

Honesty about the job

The role we describe is the role that exists. Real pay, real hours, real location. No candidate should arrive and find a different job waiting.

02

Written terms, always

Every candidate and every employer receives our terms in writing before anything is agreed. If something is unclear, we explain it again.

03

Licensed employers only

We introduce candidates to UK businesses holding a valid sponsor licence. We check that status before we put anyone forward.

04

Support after arrival

A placement is not finished when the plane lands. We stay reachable through the first months, for both sides.

How we operate. EcoEarth Ltd is a recruitment agency. We do not sponsor visas ourselves — we connect skilled workers with UK employers who hold a valid Home Office sponsor licence, and that employer issues the Certificate of Sponsorship. We will always tell you clearly who your employer would be before you commit to anything.
